About

CineGrid is a catalogue of external videos. Videos are hosted on MEGA; nothing is stored or streamed by this app. Some titles may show a supported advertisement before the MEGA link opens.

Privacy

CineGrid itself collects no phone number, name or analytics, and sets no trackers. Saved items and theme live in your browser's local storage. The ad provider (Monetag) may process data under its own policy while an ad is shown. MEGA links are not private: anyone holding a link can open or share it.

A supported advertisement may be required before the external video (hosted on MEGA) opens.
